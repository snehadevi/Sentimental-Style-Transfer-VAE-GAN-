# Sentimental-Style-Transfer-VAE-GAN


Variational and probabilistic deep learning methods remain underexplored in NLP due to their complexity. Our study investigates sentimental style transfer using these methods, focusing on the hybridization of Variational Autoencoders (VAE) and Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) to separate content reconstruction and sentiment modification tasks, achieving improved content preservation and sentiment alignment.

The proposed hybrid architecture leverages the VAE for accurate sentence reconstruction and the GAN discriminator to enforce sentiment control, demonstrating superior performance compared to prior models. The approach also enables versatile applications, such as paraphrasing through sentence reconstruction or generating novel sentences via prior sampling.
